03 / Strategy

Our environmental strategy towards 2050

Transition strategy following a structured framework: improve current operations, transition fleet standards, and deploy zero-emission options.

2030

Efficiency and compliance

Short-term goal focusing on operational measures to reduce emissions and avoid CII D/E ratings.

  • Optimise vessel speed and voyage planning.
  • Implement weather routing and just-in-time arrival.
  • Maintain clean hulls and propellers.
  • Use shore power at EU ports and trial sustainable biofuel blends.
2030 focus: Operational emission cuts and compliance with stringent IMO/EU limits.
2040

Fleet renewal and alternative fuels

Structural transition shifting investments toward new vessel designs and low-carbon propulsion.

  • Retire least efficient units failing EEXI thresholds.
  • Invest in newbuildings capable of utilizing green methanol, ammonia, or advanced biofuels.
  • Scale up shore power application and long-term clean fuel agreements.
2040 focus: Substantially renewed fleet with drastically reduced lifecycle GHG intensity.
2050

Near-zero emission shipping

Long-term alignment with international maritime net-zero pathways.

  • Operate entirely on scalable zero- or near-zero-GHG fuels.
  • Maintain top-tier CII performance (A/B ratings).
  • Minimize exposure to carbon pricing and penalty mechanisms.
2050 focus: Fully net-zero compatible transport system.
04 / Methodology

Calculation documentation & Regulatory frameworks

Documentation of formulas and compliance models implemented for CII, EEXI, EU ETS, and FuelEU Maritime.

01 — Operational CII & Ratings

Calculated via annual emissions relative to transport work capacity.

CII_attained = CO₂_annual(g) / (DWT × D_annual)

Compared against required targets with a 5% reduction factor ($z=5\%$).

02 — EEXI Index

Design energy efficiency indexing based on reference speeds and installed power on HFO.

EEXI = CO₂ / (Capacity × V_ref)

03 — EU ETS Compliance

Allowance surrenders scaled for 100% phase-in by 2026 at an assumed carbon price of €80/t.

EUAs_required = CO₂_covered × Phase_percentage Carbon_cost = EUAs_required × EUA_price

04 — FuelEU Maritime

Compares lifecycle GHG energy intensity against baseline thresholds, generating deficits for HFO-reliant ships.

GHG intensity = Total lifecycle GHG / Energy used
